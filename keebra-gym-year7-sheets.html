Neuromuscular Control — 8 minutes
Keebra Park GRLA — Year 7 · LEVEL 1.1
Phase 1 — ESTABLISH
Runs before every session
Gym or field
Why this runs first
Fifteen minutes is the evidence-based dose; eight minutes done every session beats fifteen done sometimes. Slow and deliberate — this is motor learning, not conditioning. It is the last thing you cut and the first thing you keep, and it runs in full even on an amber day.
1 · Raise90 seconds
ExerciseDoseCoaching cue
1 Skip / bike / 10m shuttles 60–90 sec Just enough to warm, not to tire.
2 Inch Worm 3 reps Walk the hands out, hips stay level.
3 Walking Lunge 5 e/s Tall chest, back knee under the hip.
2 · The spine every session · all levels · no exceptions
ExerciseDoseCoaching cue
1 Low Box Step-Down (15–35 cm) 2 x 6 e/s Lower slowly, knee over the 2nd toe, touch light, return.
2 Jump & Stick Landing 5 jumps Land soft and quiet, hips back, HOLD 2 seconds.
3 Neck Iso Holds — 4-way 1 x 10 sec e/way Front, back, both sides. Brace before contact.
3 · Phase 1 — ESTABLISH pick 3 per session · rotate so the block covers all six
ExerciseDoseCoaching cue
1 Supine Heel Slide Walkout 2 x 6 e/s Push the heel into the floor, slide slow, hips do not drop.
2 Single Leg Glute Bridge 2 x 8 e/s Drive through the heel, squeeze at the top, hips level.
3 Clamshell 2 x 10 e/s Hips stacked, lift the top knee without rolling the pelvis back.
4 Prone Hip Extension 2 x 8 e/s Squeeze the glute BEFORE the leg lifts. Slow.
5 Dead Bug 2 x 6 e/s Lower back stays flat on the floor, breathe out on the effort.
6 Single Leg Balance 2 x 30 sec e/s Soft knee, eyes forward, adjust without looking at your feet.
Phase 1 — ESTABLISHLvl 1.1–1.3 · bodyweight, on the floor, slow
Phase 2 — LOADLvl 1.4–2.2 · bands, more reps, tempo
Phase 3 — INTEGRATELvl 2.3–3.2 · single-leg demand, unstable surface
Phase 4 — EXPRESSLvl 3.3–3.8 · reactive, at speed, full intent
Phase follows movement quality, not age or year level. She moves up when she is controlled and stable at the phase she is in — not when the calendar says so.
Who needs watching — knee position, pelvis level, quality under fatigue
Equipment: bands (light/medium/heavy) · low box 15–35 cm · foam pad CARE · ACCOUNTABILITY · RESPECT · EXCELLENCE

Moving a group up a phase
An athlete moves up when she is controlled and stable at the phase she is in — knee tracking over the second toe, pelvis level, quality holding when she is tired. Not when five weeks have passed. Two athletes in the same session can be in different phases, and often should be. The spine (Box Step-Down, Jump & Stick, Neck Iso) does not change: it runs at every phase, every session.
