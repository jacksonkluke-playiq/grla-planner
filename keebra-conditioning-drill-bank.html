Acceleration Wall Drill
Wall or partner
 3 x 10mYr 7Yr 8Yr 9 Lean on a wall or partner, hips tall. Punch the ground away, shin angle out not down. Short, fast steps.
Teaches the accel shin angle before it is asked for at speed.
Build-Up Sprint
Cones
 3 x 20mYr 7Yr 8Yr 9 20m lane, build through the first 10m. Build, do not launch. Tall, relaxed jaw and hands.
Exposes top-end mechanics without a max effort cold.
Jog-to-Stick Decel
Cones
 4 x 10mYr 7Yr 8Yr 9 Jog 10m into a cone, stop dead and hold 2 sec. Drop the hips, short braking steps, knee over the 2nd toe.
QRL 5.1: teach deceleration before acceleration. Decel is where the ACL goes.
I-Drill at 50%
6 cones
 3 runs @ 50%Yr 8Yr 9 Five cones, 5m apart. Touch every cone, always running forward. Plant the foot CLOSE to the body, rotate the hip through. No wide step.
QRL coach manual. A wide braking step is the highest-risk cut pattern in female athletes.
M-Drill at 50–75%
6 cones
 3 runs, build 50→75%Yr 9 M shape, 5m legs. Forward running throughout, hip-driven turns. Hip first, foot follows. Speed is earned by mechanics, not assigned.
QRL coach manual. Repeated direction change under forward momentum.

3-2-1 Continuous Game
Cones, whistle
 3 x 7 min, 2 min betweenYr 7Yr 8Yr 9 50m area. Coach calls every change: '3-2-1 sprint', '3-2-1 drop and jog'. On 'drop', controlled to the ground and back up fast, then straight into the next call.
QRL's own session. Coach controls the walk:jog:sprint ratio, so intensity is dialled live.
Ball-in-Hand Continuous
Balls, cones
 4 x 5 min, 90 sec betweenYr 7Yr 8Yr 9 Grid 30x30. Continuous handling at stride, two-pass minimum before a turn. Hands early, eyes up. Skill does not drop because the lungs are working.
Gabbett 2006: skill-based games match traditional running for conditioning in rugby league.
Extensive Shuttle Tempo
Cones
 2 x 8 reps, 1:1Yr 8Yr 9 60m out and back at ~70%, walk the same distance back. Smooth and repeatable. If rep 8 is slower than rep 1, you went too hard.
Builds the aerobic base that lets her recover between the hard efforts (QRL 4.2).

2 cones
 3 x 4 min, 60 sec betweenYr 7Yr 8 Two cones 20m apart, run a figure 8 around them continuously. Lean into the turn from the hip, not the ankle. Same pace each lap.

15:60 Repeat Sprint
Cones, timer
 8–12 repsYr 7Yr 8Yr 9 15 seconds maximal effort, 60 seconds active recovery (walk, do not stand). Full intent every rep. The recovery is a walk, not a rest.
QRL 4.1 prescribes exactly this: 10–30 sec efforts at 1:3 to 1:5.
10/20/30 Shuttle + Skill
Cones, ball
 6 efforts, 1:3Yr 7Yr 8Yr 9 Sprint to 10m, back; 20m, back; 30m, back. Catch and pass at the line each time. The pass still has to be good on the last shuttle. That is the whole point.
Repeat effort with a skill under fatigue, which is how the game actually asks for it.
Sled Push-Pull Relay
Sled + 10kg
 3 full efforts eachYr 8Yr 9 Cones at 0/10/20/30m, one sled per four, 10kg plate. Push 10m, pull back 10m, push 30m, hand over. Push: drive the ground away, high knees, eyes up. Pull: stay low, drive the heels.
QRL's own session. Runs alongside skills — rotate four out, they return fatigued.

3-Effort RHIE Bout
Tackle tube, cones · contact
 6 bouts, 90 sec betweenYr 8Yr 9 Sprint 15m, get up off the deck, sprint 10m, hit the tackle tube, sprint 10m. All inside ~20 sec. Three efforts, no gap between them. The last 10m is the one that matters.
A rugby league RHIE bout is 3+ efforts inside 21 sec (Austin, Gabbett & Jenkins). Max bouts run 49–64 sec.
Wrestle + Shuttle
Mats or soft ground · contact
 4 rounds eachYr 9 Even pairs. Sprint 10m, 20m, 30m back to the line, then wrestle for 30 sec. Wrestle from a braced trunk and a strong neck, never a loose one.
Wrestling inside games raises repeat-effort demand while lowering running load (Johnston et al.).
Tackle Tube Continuous
Tackle tube · contact
 4 x 1 min, 90 sec betweenYr 7Yr 8Yr 9 Pairs. Run to a standing tube, tackle it to ground, carry it back. Repeat for the minute. Both arms wrap, get low, cheek to the right side. Technique first, speed second.
Tests the ability to keep making tackles once tired — the moment technique slips is the moment to stop.
Tackle Shuttle
Tackle bag · contact
 3 x 8 tacklesYr 7Yr 8Yr 9 Face down 3m from a bag. Get up, stay low, tackle, alternating lead shoulders. Alternate shoulders every rep. Chin off chest, eyes open, shoulder through.
Builds both shoulders. Female athletes are often one-sided in the tackle.
Off-Side Game + Contact Bout
Balls, cones, pads · contact
 2 x 10 min halvesYr 8Yr 9 Small-sided off-side game. Every 2 min the coach calls a contact bout. Running intensity must not drop when the contact goes in. That is the trainable thing.
Johnston et al.: running intensity falls as contact efforts rise — so train them together.

Defensive Line Speed + Retreat
Cones
 5 x 45 sec, 90 sec betweenYr 8Yr 9 Line of 5. Up together 10m on the call, retreat 10m, repeat. Up as one. The slowest player sets the line, so talk.
Game-specific repeat effort that also rehearses line discipline.
Game Simulation Sets
Balls, cones · contact
 4 sets, 2 min betweenYr 8Yr 9 Full set of six with a kick, then the chase and retreat. Live tempo. Last tackle discipline. Chase hard, reset the line, talk.

Two-Pass Transition Game
Balls, cones
 4 x 90 secYr 7Yr 8Yr 9 Minimum two passes before contact or touch. Turnover = instant transition. React to the turnover. The first three steps after it decide the set.
Trains the transition moment, where repeat-effort demand spikes.
Kick-Chase and Retreat
Balls, cones
 6 repsYr 8Yr 9 Kick, full line chase 20m, retreat 10m, reset. Chase as a line, not as individuals.
Rehearses the highest-speed phase of the game.
Numbers-Up Attack v Defence
Balls, cones
 6 setsYr 7Yr 8Yr 9 4v3 or 5v4 attack. Swap after each set. Attack: hold the defender. Defence: slide and talk.
